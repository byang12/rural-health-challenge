Course 1 · Topic 5 of 6

Verify captures and document gaps

Reading time: about 4 minutes

In this topic

Make a source-based review decision for each captured value, while keeping missing values, conflicting accounts, and the original AI output visible.

For your project: you add a first review record and a list of unresolved issues for Course 2, without cleaning away any evidence.

Key terms
Review decision
What you did with a captured value: ACCEPT it, CORRECT it from the source, or REJECT_VALUE_KEEP_GAP (reject the value but keep the information need visible).
Evidence status
What the supplied material supports, such as SUPPORTED or NOT_FOUND (see Topic 3).
Issue
Something still unresolved, such as two sources that disagree. It is recorded and passed on, not hidden.
Missing code
A short local code for why a value is empty, such as UNK (unknown) or NC (not collected), stored with a plain-language reason.

What verification is, and is not

Verification asks whether a captured value has the meaning and support the collection specification requires. It is not a contest to prove that AI always fails.

  • A supported value can be accepted unchanged.
  • An extraction error can be corrected when the source gives the correct information.
  • An unsupported value should be rejected, while the need for that information stays visible.

For the small teaching excerpt, review every captured row and every substantive field. A general statement that “the AI was checked” is not enough.

Check against the source, not another answer

Compare the output with the source, not with another fluent answer. Check:

  • the identifier and the requested meaning;
  • the value, and its unit or date format;
  • the source location, scope, and evidence category;
  • omissions: did the tool include every requested checklist item or event statement?

AI can help flag differences, but make the acceptance decision from the actual material. A second AI response can suggest a check; it cannot replace looking at the source that is supposed to support the value.

Three worked judgments

Worked review judgments for the three problems
SituationWhat to recordWhy
Problem 1: PKT-A’s cover sheet says a handoff summary is attached, but the supplied files contain only the cover sheet.NOT_FOUND for the summary item, with a note explaining the difference.The summary is not in the supplied material. Do not conclude that it was never written.
Problem 2: an assistant adds a completed visit for PKT-B, but the log only shows a planned visit after the cutoff.Reject the invented completion date and keep the missing completion as a documented gap.A planned visit is not evidence of a completed visit.
Problem 3: the rural coordinator’s card names the receiving scheduler as the follow-up owner; the scheduler’s card names the rural coordinator.Accept both as reported accounts and open a conflict issue.Each account matches its own source. Disagreement does not make either one false, and the AI’s preferred reading is not policy.

Course 2 needs to know both the values and the unresolved relationship between them.

Keep three things separate

Use separate fields for evidence status, missing reason, and review decision. A supported statement can still conflict with another supported statement, and a blank date is not zero.

The project proposes local missing codes such as UNK for unknown and NC for not collected. These are not official DDI codes. Record a plain-language reason and use the code that actually fits the gap. An expected event that is not documented may belong in the issue list rather than in an invented event row.

Keep the original; record every change

Keep the original source and the AI output unchanged. In the reviewed copy, record the previous value, the replacement if the source supports one, the source location, the reviewer, and the reason for the change.

Course 1 work

Correcting an extraction against its source.

Course 2 work

Standardizing datasets, resolving duplicates, calculating time intervals, and deciding how tables join.

A clearly recorded unresolved issue can travel with a usable handoff. An issue that hides what a value means or where it came from must be fixed before the value is accepted.

AI instruction for this stage

Prompt to adapt: check the capture

Compare the candidate capture with the supplied source text and reviewed variable definitions. Flag mismatches, omitted items, unsupported values, and conflicting accounts. For each flag, identify the row or field and exact supporting location. Suggest ACCEPT, CORRECT, or REJECT_VALUE_KEEP_GAP, but do not overwrite the original output or guess a resolution. Keep source support separate from cross-source agreement.

Practice activity

Try it: Workpad C

Record one acceptance or correction and one unresolved gap in Workpad C. Link each decision to a source location, and do not change the original output.

Check your understanding

Two role cards assign receipt follow-up to different roles. Each extracted answer matches its card. What is the correct review action?

  1. Ask AI to pick the more likely role.
  2. Accept both as reported accounts and flag the unresolved responsibility conflict.
  3. Delete both answers.
Show the answer

Answer: B. Whether a source supports a value and whether two sources agree are different questions. Keep the accounts, and note the clarification needed.

Choosing a likely owner (A) invents an operating rule. Deleting both (C) loses evidence. The conflict is part of the handoff, not an extraction failure.
