Rural Health Data Collection

Explore why rural health needs different kinds of information, find an official public source, and learn to recognize what evidence can and cannot tell us.

Preparation lead: Fabricio Gutierrez Juarez

This is a short, beginner-friendly tutorial. You do not need any technical background.

Start the tutorial Available soon

The tutorial opens in a new tab, on another website. Close that tab to come back to this page.

What you will learn

  • Understand: why rural healthcare questions need local context.
  • Find: how to explore CDC PLACES without coding.
  • Check: how to describe limitations and avoid unsupported claims.

What is inside

Video-led learning, short clickable explanations, real public data, and quick checks.
