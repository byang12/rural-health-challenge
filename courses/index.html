How to use the courses

Read the learning goals and worked example, try the short exercise, and use the self-check questions to review your reasoning. Apply the methods relevant to your team’s question to the final proposal and supporting artifact.

The practice activities are learning aids. They do not create six additional final deliverables. Your required outputs remain the short proposal, one supporting analysis or demonstration, and the team presentation.

Shared practice case

A fictional outpatient clinic processes administrative referral packets. A hypothetical assisted workflow could reduce handling time but requires a tool subscription and staff review. The case helps you separate staff capacity, recurring spending, quality, and uncertainty.

Fictional teaching data

All practice numbers and hypothetical outputs are invented for teaching. They do not describe a participating clinic, measure the performance of an AI product, or establish a financial benefit.

Getting help

For questions about a source or data field, consult the data and GIS lead through the program coordinator. For a calculation or workflow comparison, bring your input assumptions and expected result to an office hour. For a clinical or partner-context question, request clarification through the IRHR lead.
