Frequently Asked Questions

Participation and teams

Who can participate?

Review the eligibility statement on the Apply page and in the application form. Students from different disciplines can contribute through data work, business reasoning, healthcare context, community understanding, design, or communication.

Do I need to know how to code?

Advanced coding is not required. A spreadsheet, map, simple scenario comparison, or small workflow demonstration can satisfy the supporting-artifact requirement. The courses provide a common starting point.

Can I apply without a complete team?

The application instructions will state whether individual applications, existing teams, or both are accepted. The challenge itself uses teams of three. Contact the program coordinator if you need clarification about team formation.

Project scope and AI

Do we need to develop an entire clinic plan?

Your required project focuses on one problem and one proposed improvement. You should explain enough about the service and operating setting to assess the idea. A full clinic business plan or new facility design is an optional extension.

Are there six separate course assignments to submit?

The course activities are guided practice. Your final requirements are the short proposal, one supporting analysis or demonstration, and the presentation. Reuse the relevant course work in those outputs.

Must the recommendation itself use AI?

AI can support your investigation or the proposed workflow. For example, it may help organize evidence or explore assumptions even when the recommendation concerns staffing or appointment timing. Explain the role of AI and how important outputs were checked. A custom AI application is optional.

What if our check finds little benefit from AI?

Explain the result and its implications. A careful assessment can support a recommendation to revise the task, simplify the approach, or seek better evidence.

Data and evidence

Will we receive data?

Organizers provide a case brief, starting data, common assumptions, and templates. The course practice case uses fictional information. Additional local data collection is optional and coordinated through the program.

Can we interview a clinic or community organization?

Discuss the purpose with the organizers first. They coordinate partner requests and the appropriate arrangements. You can complete the required project using supplied materials and documented assumptions.

What if important information is missing?

State the gap, explain why it matters, and show how a plausible range or alternative assumption could affect the recommendation. Request clarification through the program when the gap prevents a useful comparison.

Does saved staff time count as a financial saving?

Explain what actually changes. Freed time can provide capacity for other work while payroll spending remains the same. A cash saving, added spending, staff capacity, and community benefit should be identified separately.

Are the fictional practice numbers real clinic results?

All numbers and hypothetical outputs in the practice case are invented for teaching. They do not describe a local partner or establish the performance of an AI product. Use your assigned case evidence for the final recommendation.

Dates, recognition, and contact

What are the dates and time commitment?

Applications are due November 20, 2026. Training begins after that date, and evaluation takes place in January 2027. Session dates, delivery format, attendance expectations, and workload guidance will be shared before participants accept a place.

Will there be prizes or certificates?

The winning team will be announced in January 2027. Any additional awards, recognition, or completion arrangements will be posted when confirmed.

Will our work be shared publicly?

Any public showcase or publication arrangements will be communicated separately. Ask the organizers before sharing restricted partner materials. Project ownership and sharing terms should be reviewed in the final participant information.

Who should I contact for help?

Contact the program coordinator (email address to be posted) for application, schedule, access, or participation questions. Rei Wu coordinates program queries and connects teams with the appropriate lead. Share any access needs privately with the coordinator so suitable arrangements can be discussed.
