Resources and Templates

The practice case, templates for your three outputs, and public data starting points.

Start with the shared case

Use your assigned case brief, starter data, and common assumptions before collecting additional information. Check source dates, units, geography, and definitions. Keep a visible distinction between observed inputs and assumptions.

Fictional teaching data

The public practice case is fictional and can help you learn the methods before the final partner or county materials are released.

Public data starting points

SourceWhat it may contributeWhat to check
US Census American Community Survey Population, income, insurance, housing, employment, and transportation context. Estimate period, geographic unit, uncertainty, and the population represented.
CDC PLACES Local population health estimates and related measures. These are modeled population estimates. They do not directly predict a clinic’s patient volume.
HRSA Health Center Data Health-center locations and service information. Coverage and definitions; this is not a complete live directory of all local providers.
Alabama Department of Public Health data Alabama and county health information and linked resources. The underlying data year may differ from the website update date.

Select sources that answer your question. A large number of datasets does not by itself strengthen a recommendation.

Simple analysis tools

A spreadsheet is often sufficient for a small capacity or cost comparison. A map can help when location or travel is central. A supplied simulation can help examine how an assumption changes an outcome. Use the simplest tool that can show your reasoning clearly.

Record evidence and AI use within your work

For each important input, state its source, date, unit, and whether it is observed, estimated, or assumed. For an important AI-supported step, explain the task, the tool used, how you checked the result, and any correction or limitation that mattered.

Include this information in the proposal or supporting artifact. A separate AI log or data report is optional.

Requesting clarification

Send a focused question to the program coordinator (email address to be posted) or use the office-hour arrangements posted on the Timeline page. Organizers coordinate requests to local partners so teams receive comparable support.
