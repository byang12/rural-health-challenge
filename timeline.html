Timeline

From the October 2026 announcement to the January 2027 final evaluation.

Program calendar

  1. October 30, 2026

    Announcement

    Challenge information and application guidance become available.

  2. November 20, 2026

    Application deadline

    Submit the application using the published form.

  3. After November 20, 2026

    Orientation and training

    Selected teams receive their case, resources, and training schedule.

  4. Between orientation and the January review

    Team project work

    Teams narrow their question, develop a comparison, and prepare their recommendation.

  5. January 2027

    Final evaluation and winner announcement

    Teams present their work and receive feedback.

Exact training, submission, presentation, and notification dates will be posted when confirmed. The final participant schedule will state submission times and the applicable time zone.

How your project develops

Begin by agreeing on one question with your team and mentor. Use the collection and curation courses to identify and check the information that matters. Then establish current practice and compare it with one proposed improvement.

The service-planning and AI courses help you assess feasibility and test relevant assumptions. The communication course helps you turn the same work into your short proposal, supporting artifact, and presentation.

Feedback during the challenge

Teams will have access to shared mentoring and program feedback. Office-hour dates and the process for requesting case clarification will be posted in the participant schedule. Bring a specific question, the evidence you have, and the assumption or decision you need help with.

The project period includes the end-of-year academic break. Teams should use the final schedule to agree on a manageable work plan and communicate availability early.

After the final review

Promising recommendations may be considered for further discussion or validation with partners. Any next phase will have its own scope, responsibilities, and arrangements. The January submission should identify the most useful next step.
