Template

Improvement proposal template

A frame for your 4–5 page proposal. Replace each prompt with your own text, and keep every number identical to your supporting artifact.

How to use this template
  • Download the Word version, or copy the headings into a shared document your team already uses.
  • Keep the proposal to 4–5 pages, excluding references. The suggested lengths add up to about 4½ pages.
  • Replace every prompt and [bracketed placeholder], and delete the guidance before you submit.
  • Use the same inputs, units, and definitions as your supporting artifact (TeamName_Analysis).
  • Name the file TeamName_Proposal.

1. Executive summary and problem statement

Suggested length: ½–1 page

Recommendation in three sentences

Open the proposal with this. Sentence one states the change, sentence two the strongest evidence, and sentence three the next validation step.

We propose [the change] so that [who] can [what improves]. Under [the inputs, labeled observed, estimated, or assumed], it would [the main measured or modeled result], while [the main cost or burden]. As a first step, we recommend [a small validation test] to check [the assumption that matters most].

Problem statement

  • Which clinic or service is affected, and who feels the problem?
  • What does current practice (the baseline) look like?
  • What constraints does the clinic work under: staff, hours, budget, distance, or technology?
  • Why does the problem matter to the local community?

2. Proposed solution and supporting evidence

Suggested length: about 1 page

How the change works

  • Who does the work, and what changes in their routine?
  • What tool, resource, or partner is needed?
  • Who oversees the change?

A short before-and-after description or a simple diagram is enough.

References

List every source you cite: author or organization, year, title, and link. References do not count toward the page limit.

Before you submit

  • The proposal answers your original question, and the recommendation is in the first paragraph.
  • Every input has a value, unit, type, and source.
  • Every number matches your supporting artifact and slides. Your Team Log’s change table shows nothing left to update.
  • Staff capacity, cash costs, and community access are described separately.
  • The weakest assumption, the point where it would change the answer, and a small next step are named.
  • AI use and the checks you ran are described within the proposal.
  • The proposal is 4–5 pages, excluding references, and the file is named TeamName_Proposal.
