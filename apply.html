Who can apply

The eligibility statement will be posted here and in the application form.

Students from computing, data science, business, health, engineering, social work, public health, communication, and other relevant fields are encouraged to consider the challenge within the published eligibility requirements.

Advanced programming experience is not required. Teams can use shared tools and a simple spreadsheet, map, or workflow analysis as their supporting artifact. A willingness to learn, contribute, and explain your reasoning is important.

Team formation

The challenge uses teams of three. Each team should combine perspectives that help it understand the evidence, operating constraints, and community implications of its question.

The application instructions will state whether individual applications, existing teams, or both are accepted.

Prepare for the application

Be ready to provide your name and contact information, institution and field of study, areas of interest, relevant experience, and availability. A brief statement should explain what you hope to learn and what you could contribute to a team. Include team information if the final application instructions request it.

Applications are reviewed by the organizing team, which will communicate next steps and team arrangements. The number of available places and notification timing will be stated in the application instructions.

Participation expectations

Selected participants should be prepared to engage with the short courses, contribute to team work, use data responsibly, and take part in the final presentation. The detailed session schedule, delivery format, attendance expectations, and workload guidance will be provided before participants accept a place.

Recognition

The winning team will be announced in January 2027. Further details about recognition or any awards will be posted when confirmed.

Questions before applying

Contact the program coordinator at the program email address (to be posted) for questions about eligibility, team formation, participation, or access to the application.
