Tutorial 5 of 6

AI Applications and Scenario Testing

What AI can and cannot do in rural health care, which data is safe to use with it, and how to start using a free AI assistant safely.

Preparation lead: Jiacheng Cao

This is a short tutorial with no coding. The main path takes about 50 to 55 minutes, including about 16 minutes of video. The open questions and the hands-on practice take about 30 minutes more. You do not need any coding or health background.

Start the tutorial Available soon

The tutorial opens in a new tab. Close that tab to come back to this page.

What you will learn

  • Understand: what AI is, the two main kinds, and four warning signs to watch for.
  • Compare: five ways rural health care differs from city health care, and why each one matters for AI tools.
  • Test: why an AI tool should be tested locally before anyone relies on it.
  • Protect: what is safe to share with an AI tool, and why removing names is not enough.
  • Try: how to open the free version of Gemini, and four habits for using any AI tool safely.

What is inside

Six short videos with captions and transcripts, five short readings, quick quizzes, and open questions with model answers. The hands-on practice in Section 5 uses the free version of Gemini, which needs a Google account.

The videos are narrated by an AI-generated voice, not a human.
