How to use the tutorials

Open a tutorial page and press Start. Watch the short videos, or read their text, and try the quick questions.

Apply the methods relevant to your team’s question to the final proposal and supporting artifact.

The practice activities are learning aids. They do not create six additional final deliverables. Your required outputs remain the short proposal, one supporting analysis or demonstration, and the team presentation.

Tutorial materials and sessions

Each tutorial page tells you what to expect and has a button that starts the tutorial. Examples marked as made-up are for teaching only. Any lesson recordings, slides, and live-session links will be added when available. Final session dates and delivery arrangements will be posted on the Timeline page.

Getting help

For questions about a source or data field, consult the data and GIS lead through the program coordinator. For a calculation or workflow comparison, bring your input assumptions and expected result to an office hour. For a clinical or partner-context question, request clarification through the IRHR lead.
