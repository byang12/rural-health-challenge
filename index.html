The challenge

A focused project with practical support

  • How could a rural clinic use its staff time more effectively?
  • Could a different appointment schedule improve access?
  • Could a clearer referral process reduce administrative work?

The IRHR–ALAAI Rural Health Transformation Challenge invites students from different disciplines to investigate questions like these. Teams of three use a county or provider case, shared data, six short courses, and mentoring to develop an evidence-based recommendation.

The first round will draw on cases from three West Alabama counties. Participating counties and case partners will be announced when confirmed.

What you will do

Choose one focused problem from your assigned case. Check the relevant evidence, compare current practice with one proposed improvement, and explain the costs, benefits, assumptions, and next step. You may use AI to support your analysis or proposed workflow and will explain how you checked its important outputs.

What you will submit

  • Improvement proposalA short improvement proposal of 4–5 pages, excluding references.
  • Supporting analysis or demonstrationOne supporting analysis or demonstration, such as a spreadsheet, map, simple simulation, or small workflow example.
  • Team presentationA presentation of 5–7 slides, delivered in eight minutes, followed by questions.

These outputs describe the same project. The courses help you build them progressively.

Your experience

Why participate

Practice working across disciplines, using evidence to make a decision, assessing the usefulness of AI, and explaining your recommendation to healthcare and community audiences. Promising recommendations may advance to further partner discussion or a small validation activity.
